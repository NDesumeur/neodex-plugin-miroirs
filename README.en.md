# Mirrors

A pixel-art puzzle: turn the mirrors to guide the laser to every crystal.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Controls

- **Mouse**: click a mirror to turn it.
- **Keyboard**: **arrows** move the cursor, **Enter** or **Space** turns the mirror. **H**: hint · **R**: restart.
- **Gamepad**: d-pad or stick moves, **A** turns, **Y** gives a hint, **LB** restarts.

## How it works

- Each mirror bounces the laser at a right angle. **Walls** stop it, **crystals** let it through.
- Light **all crystals** at once to finish the level.
- 80 levels, all solvable.
- The laser shines (a halo, a white core) and throws **sparks** on every mirror it hits.
- **Full screen**: the button of the same name; Escape to leave it. Otherwise the game takes all the room the window leaves.

## Advanced levels

From level 41, new elements arrive:

- **colour filters** (red, green, blue): the laser going through takes their colour;
- **coloured crystals**: they only light up with a laser of their colour; the white crystal lights up with any;
- the **prism** (from level 45): it splits the laser into two beams, left and right; you then have to guide both.

## Data and sync

Levels and stars are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
